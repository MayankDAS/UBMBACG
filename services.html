Services

Companies come to consultants because they need to make a decision, solve a problem, or move an initiative forward. We work with organizations to understand their challenges and develop practical, tailored recommendations that help them move forward. Each client works with a dedicated team of at least three MBA and MS consultants.

01 / START WITH THE BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Growth & markets

Where should we grow? Which customers or markets should we prioritize?

Operational performance

Where are costs, delays, or manual work holding us back?

Data & decisions

What does our data tell us, and what should we do differently?

AI

Where can AI help, what is feasible, and how should we start?

Business model & strategy

How should we position, compete, or adapt to change?

Execution & readiness

What are the next steps, resources, risks, and measures of success?

Engagement scope is tailored to client needs and matched to available team capabilities.

AI is a capability, not the answer to every problem.

We help organizations move from AI curiosity to informed, practical next steps.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand business goals, workflows, pain points, and constraints.

  2. 02

    Assess

    Identify AI use cases and compare them with non-AI alternatives.

  3. 03

    Prioritize

    Evaluate potential value, feasibility, data readiness, cost, and risk.

  4. 04

    Plan

    Develop a roadmap, pilot concept, success measures, and next steps.

Responsible by design: AI outputs require human review. Our recommendations account for privacy, data security, accuracy, governance, and each client’s operating context. Implementation support is scoped to the engagement and the team’s verified capabilities; production deployment is not assumed.

03 / HOW AN ENGAGEMENT WORKS

A structured path from challenge to action.

We accept client projects twice a year, for spring and fall. Spring projects run from February through April.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Align on the business question, context, and desired outcome.

  2. 02

    Structure

    Define scope, workstreams, timeline, and deliverables.

  3. 03

    Analyze

    Conduct research, assess data, test assumptions, and compare options.

  4. 04

    Recommend

    Prioritize findings and develop actionable recommendations.

  5. 05

    Plan next steps

    Outline owners, milestones, resources, risks, and success measures.

Each engagement has a defined scope and agreed deliverables and includes at least 4 meetings between your team and our consultants.

04 / WORKING TOGETHER

What clients can expect

  • A clear understanding of the business problem
  • An agreed scope, timeline, and deliverables
  • Structured research and analysis
  • Regular progress communication
  • A final presentation and practical next steps

What makes a project successful

  • A defined decision or problem to address
  • Access to appropriate, approved information
  • A designated client point of contact
  • Timely feedback and alignment
  • Realistic expectations for scope and implementation

Confidentiality and data

Client information is handled only under approved arrangements. Sensitive data, AI tools, and external sharing require appropriate review and authorization.
