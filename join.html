Real Consulting. Real Clients. Real Leadership.

UB MBA Consulting Group is a student-led consulting organization that partners with businesses and nonprofit organizations throughout Western New York. Work on projects that make a real impact, not classroom simulations. MBA students and master’s students across the School of Management (including MIS, MSA, MSF, and more) are welcome, and no prior consulting experience is needed.

02 / WAYS TO GET INVOLVED

Consultant

Join a client team and contribute research, analysis, and recommendations.

Project Manager

Lead a real consulting team. Manage client communication, coordinate consultants, track deliverables, lead meetings, and present recommendations. Project managers are assigned by engagement rather than serving as permanent executive board positions.

Executive Board

A President, four Chiefs, and eight Directors run the organization across Client Development, Consulting Delivery, Operations, and Marketing & Communications. Executive board roles are about 4–6 hours per week, from November through May.

Help build what comes next.

  • Developing new leadership systems
  • Building long term organizational sustainability
